The Challenge Gauntlet

Board, regulator, workforce and customer fire questions in turn — twelve in all. Three response cards each time: one evasive, one over-promising, one defensible. Lose three credibility and the room turns. Survive all twelve, and you face the final question with no cards at all.

Your stance (from the sample Decision Lab): Constrain. The invoice-approval agent stays at its current threshold. You declined Finance's request to raise it — because no one owns the agent, no stop condition is written, no cadence tracks the creep, and a wrongful payment is hard to reverse. Three gaps, named. Now defend it.

A strong leadership stance can do three things: explain the decision, show the evidence behind it, and name what would change the decision. Where the evidence is strong, hold the line. Where it is weak, be honest about the gap — you do not need to defend an indefensible gap, and the room can tell when you try.
