Four lenses, one decision. Trust & Stability asks if it has earned the autonomy. Failure Consequence asks who gets hurt when it's wrong. Control & Recoverability asks whether you can catch and undo it. Trade-off Reality asks what you're knowingly paying for the value. The decision is defensible when all four have been looked through — not just the comfortable ones.
