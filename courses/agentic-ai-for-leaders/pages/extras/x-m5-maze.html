The Reversibility Maze

Design a customer-payout workflow: four stages, three lanes each. Every path works when nothing goes wrong. But an error always slips in at intake — and your route decides whether it gets caught or locked in. Speed scores. Catch-points score more. There is exactly one best route. Find it.
