The Missing Log

A regulator has three questions about one automated decision. The log has fourteen fields — some answer questions, some just look busy. Each round, pick the three fields you'd stake the answer on, then watch the cross-examination. Careful: one field is a trap that sounds like evidence.

A record can look detailed without providing a defensible account of the decision. Timestamps say when. Confidence says how sure the system felt about itself. What survives cross-examination is the reasoning, the rule that fired, the triggers that were checked, and the path the customer was given.
