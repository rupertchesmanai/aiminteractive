The 3:17 Drill

A new incident, same clock discipline. The feed replays one hour in compressed time. At each timestamp: act, hold, or escalate. Act too early and you burn credibility — the next stop is slower to authorise. Act too late and you burn the window itself.

The workflow: Refund Payments agent — live five months, auto-approves customer refunds under $200, roughly 600 refunds a week. The approval memo named an executive sponsor, a Risk owner, and a pre-authorised stop on the agent path. You hold the pager.
