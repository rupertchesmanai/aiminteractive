The Cadence Budget

Governance attention is finite. You have twelve attention-points to spread across the five signals — each point buys a tighter review interval. Then twelve months run. What you watched, you catch. What you didn't, quietly rots. There is exactly one best budget. Most rooms don't find it, because most rooms skip the same signal.

The designed trap: the people signal looks optional (it pays modestly at first), but leaving it at zero triggers the month-11 incident no one can handle — capability decayed silently all year. The unique optimum funds it at weekly. Ask afterwards: what does "weekly attention to human reliance" actually look like in your organisation?

The signal most groups miss is the effect on people. The easiest signals to monitor are inside the agent. The harder ones are around it — what it changes in the team, and in the human capability needed to oversee it. A cadence is minimum, not maximum: the skill isn't watching everything, it's funding the signal you were about to skip.
