Four Doors, Six Cases

Six mini-dossiers, twenty seconds each. Every case gets one forced call: Deploy, Scale, Constrain, or Withdraw. No “it depends.” No “yes, but.” The evidence gaps are planted in the dossiers — sometimes the gap is the answer. Near-miss doors earn partial credit.
