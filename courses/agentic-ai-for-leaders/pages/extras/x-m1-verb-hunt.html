The Verb Hunt

Systems that assist leave the acting to a person. Systems that act have already taken it. One sentence at a time, faster and faster — call it before the clock dies. The verb is the tell.

The verb is the tell. Drafts, suggests, summarises, flags, recommends — assistance; a person still acts. Routes, closes, approves, pays, blocks, escalates — action; the system has already acted. The moment the verb changes, the accountability questions change with it.
Facilitator noteThe most-missed sentences are usually "flags an invoice for review" (still assistance) and "sends the apology template" (already action). Both are worth thirty seconds in the room.
