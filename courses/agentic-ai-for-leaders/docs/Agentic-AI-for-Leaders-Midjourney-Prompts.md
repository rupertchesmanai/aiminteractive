# Agentic AI for Leaders — Midjourney Prompt Set

**Companion to:** Agentic-AI-for-Leaders-Interactive-Workbook-PLAN.md
**House style:** photorealistic · shot on Hasselblad · warm cinematic light · medium-format realism · inclusive casting · `--preview` always last (v8.2).

## Casting & representation notes (applies to every prompt)

- The set deliberately varies **ethnicity, gender, age and body type** — including East Asian, South Asian, Black, Middle Eastern, Latina/Latino, Pacific Islander, Aboriginal Australian and white subjects; women, men and androgynous presentation; ages ~30 to ~68; slim, average, plus-size and tall/short builds; one wheelchair user and one person with a hearing aid, shown incidentally.
- **Authority is distributed:** across the set, the person leading the room, making the call or being deferred to is by turns an older South Asian woman, a Black man, a plus-size Pacific Islander woman, a young Latina executive, an Aboriginal Australian man, etc. No group is only ever the assistant, the observer or the junior. Disabled subjects are decision-makers, not props.
- No exaggerated features, no costume-y "diversity" styling, no tokenistic single-person-of-colour-in-background framing. Everyone is dressed as contemporary Australian corporate leadership actually dresses (smart, varied, some jackets, few ties).
- Aspect ratios: heroes `--ar 21:9` or `--ar 16:9`; cards `--ar 3:2`; portrait accents `--ar 4:5`. Keep `--preview` as the final parameter in every prompt.
- File names given per prompt for direct drop-in to `assets/media/pics/`.

No mid-gesture people, no one-off actions. Generate the still, then animate with the paired motion prompt; trim so the first and last frames match, or mirror-loop.

## Workflow notes

- Generate 2× variants per hero and pick for warmth + casting balance across the whole set (check the set as a grid before committing — the mix should read inclusive *in aggregate*, per house style).
- Watch the usual Midjourney flaws before shipping (hands, garbled text on whiteboards/documents — keep any visible writing out of focus or crop it; badge/logo hallucinations).
- Compress finals to ~200–400KB JPG at 2000px wide for `assets/media/pics/`; keep masters in `Additional Images/`.
- If any subject renders as stereotype or caricature, reroll — the standard is "colleagues you'd actually meet," never a diversity stock-photo look.
