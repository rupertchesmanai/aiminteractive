How to use this site

This workbook runs in your browser, alongside your own AI tool. Here's everything you need to know before you start.

🎛 Present vs Self-paced

The toggle in the header switches how much each page shows. Present is for the live workshop — it trims the long prose down to activity essentials and reveals facilitator timers, discussion questions and debrief points.

Self-paced shows the full teaching content — use it when revisiting the course after the workshop. Try the toggle now; this site remembers your choice.

💾 Everything autosaves — locally

Every answer box saves on every keystroke, to this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded, submitted or shared — close the tab and come back tomorrow, and your notes are exactly where you left them.

The flip side: switch to a different browser, device or private window and your notes won't follow. If you want a permanent copy, use My Teamwork Plan — it gathers everything you've written and prints to a clean A4 page.

📋 Copy chips

Every prompt and source text in the course has a Copy button — one click puts it on your clipboard, ready to paste into your LLM. No retyping. Try it:

Test prompt
Reply with exactly: "Ready for the AI for Teamwork workshop."

🤝 Work alongside your LLM

Keep this workbook and your AI tool open side-by-side — two windows or split screen works best. The rhythm of every activity is the same: read the task here, copy the prompt, work in your LLM, then bring your verified, rewritten answer back here.

The workbook captures your conclusions — the thinking happens between you and your AI tool.

🔒 Keep private data out of public tools

Before pasting anything into an LLM, strip real customer names, colleague names, confidential figures and anything commercially sensitive — or swap them for placeholders. Today's exercises use fictional scenarios and personas for exactly this reason.

Your workplace may have an approved AI tool with data protections — if so, use it. When in doubt, ask before you paste.

🧭 Finding your way

The course hub shows all eight modules with a dot marking where you've worked. Each module page has a sticky section menu, and the site remembers your last module so the hub can offer to resume.

Stuck or lost on the day? Just ask your facilitator.

Accessibility: the site works with keyboard navigation and screen readers, respects reduced-motion settings, and all text sizes respond to browser zoom. If anything gets in your way, tell your facilitator — we'd like to fix it.
