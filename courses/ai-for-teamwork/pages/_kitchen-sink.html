Reveal (staged scenario)

Module 2's cognitive-offloading and context-drift stories play out beat by beat.

Beat 1. Jordan starts using AI to draft the weekly team update. It's faster, and the first few are good.
Beat 2. Weeks pass. Jordan stops reading the source reports — the AI "knows what to say by now."
Beat 3. A key figure changes mid-month. The update ships with last month's number. Nobody catches it for two weeks.
The point. The tool didn't fail — the checking stopped. AI can support teamwork, but teams must stay cognitively engaged.
