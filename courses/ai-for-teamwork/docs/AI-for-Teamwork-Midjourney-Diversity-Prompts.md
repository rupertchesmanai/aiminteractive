# AI for Teamwork — Diversity Re-roll Prompts

Replacement prompts for **14 of the people-heavy images (~30% of the set)**, rewritten so the cast reflects a realistic modern Australian workplace: a genuine mix of ethnicities, genders, ages and body types. Same Hasselblad style, lenses and aspect ratios as the originals — save the picked output over the **same filename** in `assets/media/pics/` and nothing in the site needs to change.

## Principles baked into these prompts (and for judging outputs)

- **Specific, not token.** People are described plainly ("a Vietnamese-Australian project lead in her 40s") in ordinary professional dress — no costume-y or exoticising details, no accent props.
- **Rotate who holds the room.** People of colour, women, older and plus-size people appear as the leaders, experts and decision-makers — not only as listeners. Across the set, no group is always in the junior seat.
- **Realistic mix, not a formula.** Groups read like an actual Sydney/Melbourne office or hospital, not a stock-photo checklist. Ages 20s–60s, a range of body types, occasional visible disability treated as unremarkable.
- **Judge the output, not just the prompt.** Midjourney can drift toward caricature or stereotype — re-roll anything that exaggerates features, dresses people in clichés, or consistently puts the same demographic in the background. If skin tones render unnaturally, adjust lighting words, not the person.
- **Keep the grade consistent.** These will sit beside kept originals — same warm natural light, teal/cream environmental accents, `--style raw --v 7`. Use `--sref` on your favourite kept image to lock the palette.

## Swap workflow

1. Generate, pick your favourite from the grid, upscale.
2. **Review for representational harm before saving:** no caricatured features, no stereotyped styling, leadership roles landing as written.
3. Export JPG, compress to ~200–350KB (heroes up to ~500KB).
4. Save over the **exact existing filename** (e.g. `hub-hero-team-discussion.jpg`) in `assets/media/pics/` — the site picks it up instantly, no code changes.
5. If you keep the old version, rename it with a `-old` suffix rather than deleting; unused files don't load.
