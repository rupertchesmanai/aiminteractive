NoteMicrosoft Copilot

A quick disclaimer on Copilot

Microsoft Word was created in 1983. Copilot is based on OpenAI's GPT-5, released in August 2025. These two pieces of software weren't built for each other — if Copilot feels a bit "stuck on" and occasionally glitchy, that's because it is.

Copilot for Excel can summarise data, create pivot tables and charts, and suggest conditional formatting. Copilot for Word can re-draft text, summarise reports or generate outlines. Always use de-identified or dummy data when experimenting.

Main takeaway: ChatGPT is better in most circumstances — Copilot has potential, but it's still a separate service bolted onto 365 rather than truly integral.
