The course promise

What you'll leave with

By the end of today you'll understand the foundations of AI, be able to apply prompting techniques with confidence, and have a plan for integrating AI tools into your own work — responsibly and safely.

Foundational fluency

Explain what AI actually does, cut through the jargon, and clarify Generative vs Agentic AI.

Working skill, not theory

A plan you'll actually use

Integrate AI tools into your real workflow, with the safeguards and boundaries to use them responsibly.

The metaphor for today

You are the conductor. AI is the orchestra.

An orchestra full of brilliant musicians still needs someone deciding tempo, balance and when each section comes in. AI tools are the same — remarkably capable on their own, but they need direction, judgement and a final listen before anything goes out the door.

Today isn't about becoming a virtuoso on every instrument. It's about learning to conduct — to know which tool to reach for, how to brief it clearly, and when to trust the result versus when to double-check it yourself.

One reassurance before we start. The goal isn't to master every AI tool. It's to develop a way of thinking about AI collaboration that serves you regardless of which tools emerge tomorrow.
