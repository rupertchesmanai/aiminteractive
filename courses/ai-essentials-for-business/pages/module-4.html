Five real pairings, step by step

Each of these starts from a Deep Research report or briefing doc you've already created — expand any card for the exact steps.

ChatGPT + Gamma — research into a presentation
  1. Go to gamma.app and sign up (it's free)
  2. Click "Create new" → "Paste In Text" → "Summarize Long Text or Document"
  3. Paste in your Deep Research and click Generate
Gamma is built on Google Gemini, with similar hallucination rates to other tools — use it as a base, then export to PowerPoint to keep refining.
ChatGPT + Canva — hundreds of social posts in minutes
  1. Paste your Deep Research into a new chat, then: "Please make me ten tweets to showcase the above to my clients. Format as a TABLE with TWEET and HASHTAG"
  2. Copy into a spreadsheet, remove extra headers, save as CSV
  3. In Canva, use Bulk Create → upload your CSV → Connect Data to the right column → Generate
Works for certificates, hundreds of Instagram posts in different languages, even bulk video — as long as your data is formatted correctly.
ChatGPT + ElevenLabs — voiceover and sound effects
  1. After your Deep Research, ask ChatGPT: "I want to create a script to quickly explain the above research. Please give me ten soundbites that showcase the core points."
  2. Create a free account at elevenlabs.io, pick a voice, paste your soundbites into Text to Speech, and Generate
  3. Try Sound Effects too — type any effect and click Generate
Watch for glitches — accents can drift (an Australian voice can turn American), and stray music or words can appear. Always listen to every generation.
NotebookLM + Gamma — research context into a deck
  1. Open the NotebookLM project you created earlier
  2. Click "Briefing Doc" and let it render, then copy the contents
  3. In Gamma, create a presentation from text using that briefing doc
ChatGPT and NotebookLM are entirely different models (OpenAI vs Google) — if one isn't giving you what you need, switching models can help.
Midjourney + ChatGPT + Gamma + PowerPoint — the full stack
  1. Upload your PPT to ChatGPT and ask: "Please create ten beautiful photorealistic Midjourney prompts to create images for this presentation. Make all of them shot on a Hasselblad camera with appropriate lenses, --ar 16:9 --style raw"
  2. Paste the prompts into Midjourney and Upscale the images you like
  3. Download the upscaled images and drop them into PowerPoint
If an image is close but not quite right, hit "rerun" rather than starting over — and experiment with different cameras and lenses until you get the look you're after.
